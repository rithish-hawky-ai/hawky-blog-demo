**What is a good CTR? A good CTR clears the average for your platform and objective, and in 2026 that means 5% or higher on Google Search, 2% to 3%+ on Meta, and above 0.5% on Google Display, with no single published benchmark for YouTube.**

Click-through rate is the fastest read on whether an ad earns attention, but it is not a revenue metric. A CTR far below benchmark almost always points to a creative or targeting problem you can fix, and this guide gives 2026 CTR benchmarks by platform, industry, and YouTube, plus a method for reading and lifting your own number.

## What is CTR?

Click-through rate, or CTR, is the share of impressions that turn into clicks, calculated as clicks divided by impressions times 100. If an ad is shown 10,000 times and earns 200 clicks, the CTR is 2%. CTR is an engagement metric, not a revenue metric, because it measures how compelling the ad is at the moment of exposure rather than what happens after.

The two can move in opposite directions when a curiosity-gap hook pulls clicks that never convert, which is why CTR is a first-check number on a new creative. For the full definition and related terms, see the [CTR glossary entry](https://hawky.ai/glossary/ctr).

## How to calculate CTR (with a worked example)

CTR uses a single formula: clicks divided by impressions, multiplied by 100 to express the result as a percentage. A fashion brand that serves 50,000 impressions on Meta and collects 1,100 clicks in a week calculates 1,100 divided by 50,000, or 0.022, which is a 2.2% CTR.

Meta reports two CTR versions, all-clicks CTR and link CTR, and link CTR runs lower because it counts only clicks to the destination. Against the Meta average of 1.5% to 2.2%, confirm which version you are reading before comparing to any CTR benchmark, because the two numbers differ.

A good CTR clears the average for the platform and objective you run, because search captures intent while social and display interrupt it. The table below sets the 2026 reference points, and every figure is tied to a named source.

Google Search CTRs run highest because users arrive actively searching, Meta sits in the low single digits because it generates demand rather than capturing it, and Google Display trails everything because banners interrupt browsing.

## What is a good CTR on YouTube?

YouTube CTR is driven by your thumbnail and title rather than a paid auction, so it is not directly comparable to paid-search CTR. A viewer who discovers your video in search versus in the recommended feed sees a different creative surface, and the CTR benchmark shifts by that discovery source. YouTube does not publish a single official CTR benchmark, so treat any hard percentage as creator-reported and directional.

Read your YouTube CTR against your own channel history and your niche, not against a paid-media table. A rising trend over time is the healthier signal than a static number, and a thumbnail or title refresh that lifts CTR usually lifts the video's reach as well. The signal mechanics behind how a video is discovered are explained in YouTube's own creator help docs.

## CTR benchmarks by industry

Industry shifts the CTR benchmark as much as platform does, because visual, high-emotion categories earn clicks easily while regulated or high-consideration ones do not. On Meta, art, home decor, and fashion lead near 2.8% to 2.9%, while healthcare and finance sit below 1%, per Triple Whale, read October 2026.

| Category | Meta CTR | Note |
|----------|----------|------|
| Art, home decor, fashion | ~2.8% to 2.9% | highest-CTR categories |
| Healthcare, finance | below 1% | regulated, high-consideration |

On Google Search the pattern reverses by category: arts, entertainment, and travel land well above the cross-industry average, while B2B and legal trail the visual categories despite strong intent. Judge your CTR against your own industry row rather than the blended average, which mixes categories that behave nothing alike. ## Organic CTR versus paid CTR

Organic CTR and paid CTR measure different events, and the two numbers are not interchangeable. Organic CTR counts clicks on your page in the search results divided by the impressions your listing earned, and you can read it directly in Google Search Console. Paid CTR counts clicks on your ad, and it is the metric this article benchmarks throughout.

Some "what is a good ctr" queries are really about the organic side, so a quick read of both matters. A low organic CTR usually points to a weak title or meta description, while a low paid CTR points to creative or targeting. The two use different levers, so keep the numbers separate.

## Why a good CTR lowers what you pay

On Google Ads, CTR is a core input into Quality Score, the 1-to-10 rating that sets your cost per click and your ad rank. A stronger CTR lowers the price you pay per click, so two advertisers bidding the same amount can pay very different CPCs on CTR alone. Improving a weak CTR is often the fastest way to cut CPC without touching bids.

### How CTR feeds Quality Score

Google assigns Quality Score from three signals: expected CTR, ad relevance, and landing-page experience, all described in Google's [Quality Score help doc](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2454011). Because expected CTR is the first of the three, creative and relevance work pays back directly in a lower CPC. Meta runs a similar logic through its ad auction, where relevance signals that include CTR feed the CPM, per Meta's [ad auction help](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/190734586604825).

This cost link is why creative fatigue hurts twice: as a creative tires, CTR drops, and the falling CTR pushes CPC and CPM up at the same time. Catching that decline early protects both performance and price, and the [sample-size glossary entry](https://hawky.ai/glossary/sample-size) helps you tell a real dip from noise.

## CTR versus conversion rate

CTR measures the click, and conversion rate measures what happens after the click, so the two can move in opposite directions. An ad can win the click with a bold hook and then lose the sale because the landing page does not deliver on the promise. The healthiest accounts watch CTR and conversion rate together, because a strong ad earns the click and sets an accurate expectation the page can fulfill.

Read a low CTR with a low conversion rate as a creative or offer problem, and a high CTR with a low conversion rate as a mismatch between the ad and the landing experience. ## How to improve your CTR

When your CTR trails the benchmark for your platform, creative is usually the first lever to pull, especially on Meta and TikTok where the creative is the ad. The four levers below, in order of impact, are the ones to test before you touch targeting.

![How to improve CTR with a stronger hook, native format, keyword-to-copy match, and tighter targeting](https://cdn.hawky.ai/blog/inline/what-is-a-good-ctr/how-to-improve-ctr.webp)

- **Stronger hook.** Open with the first second of attention: a clear value proposition in the first frame or the first two words of the copy.
- **Native format.** Match the format to the placement: vertical video on Reels and TikTok, image or video on Meta, and responsive search ads on Google.
- **Keyword-to-copy match.** On Google, mirror the search term in the headline and the landing page so Quality Score and CTR rise together.
- **Tighter targeting.** Put the ad in front of people more likely to care, because relevance at the audience level lifts CTR without changing the creative.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a good CTR?

A good CTR depends on the platform and objective: 5% or higher on Google Search, 2% to 3%+ on Meta, and above 0.5% on Google Display. There is no universal number because search captures intent while social and display interrupt attention. Compare your CTR to the average for your specific platform, objective, and industry rather than a single global figure.

### What is a good CTR on YouTube?

YouTube has no single published CTR benchmark, so a good CTR is read against your own channel history and your niche, not against a paid-media table. CTR is driven by the thumbnail and title and shifts by discovery source, whether the viewer found the video in search or in the recommended feed. A rising trend over time is the healthier signal than any static number.

### Is CTR a Google ranking factor?

For paid search, yes in a specific sense: CTR is a core input into Quality Score, which affects your cost per click and your ad rank. For organic rankings, Google does not treat raw CTR as a direct ranking factor, though a strong title and meta description that lift organic CTR can support rankings indirectly. The two effects run through different mechanisms, so keep the paid and organic numbers separate.

### What is a good organic CTR?

Organic CTR is the share of search-listing impressions that turn into clicks on your page, read in Google Search Console. Because it shifts with keyword position and search volume, the honest benchmark is your own historical average and the norms for your position, not a fixed global figure. Title and meta description are the levers that move it.

### What causes a low CTR?

A low CTR is almost always a creative or relevance problem: weak hooks, generic visuals, poor ad-to-audience fit, and creative fatigue are the usual causes. On Google Search, a low CTR often signals a mismatch between the keyword, the ad copy, and the searcher's intent. Refreshing creative and tightening targeting are the fastest fixes, and the [creative-fatigue glossary entry](https://hawky.ai/glossary/creative-fatigue) explains how to spot the decline early.
